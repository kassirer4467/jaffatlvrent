Jaffa-TLV green balcony apartment

Discover the charm of Tel Aviv-Jaffa from this spacious and beautifully designed 120 sqm apartment, set on the 5th floor in one of Jaffa’s most vibrant neighborhoods.

The Apartment

The apartment comfortably accommodates up to 6 guests, with three spacious queen bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with a private ensuite, and 2.5 bathrooms in total.

A fully equipped kitchen with espresso machine, elevator, free Wi-Fi, air conditioning and heating, washer and dryer, hair dryer, flat-screen TV and private entrance provide everything you need for a comfortable stay.

An Exceptional Location

Just a 2-minute walk from the heart of the Jaffa Flea Market, surrounded by restaurants, cafés, galleries and boutiques. Bloomfield Stadium is 5 minutes away, and the nearest Mediterranean beach just 10 minutes on foot. The Bloomfield light rail station is just across the street, connecting you to central Tel Aviv in approximately 5 minutes, with easy access to Neve Tzedek, HaMesila Park, Old Jaffa and many of the city’s top attractions
